Mistworld - Milestone 2: Alphonse / Abyss loadout test

Start
  Open E:\Unreal Projects\Mistworld\Mistworld.uproject normally in Unreal.
  Use /Game/Mistworld/Maps/Lvl_CombatTest and press Play.
  Click the viewport for keyboard focus. The original kit is initially equipped.
  This remains a combat sandbox with placeholder mannequin, sword and effects.

Checkpoint loadouts
  L opens/closes the loadout panel. Click its buttons with the mouse.
  The gold circle around the initial spawn is the checkpoint (2.5 m radius).
  At the checkpoint, click Unlock Abyss (prototype test access).
  Choose Original or Abyss independently for Q, W, E and R: 16 possible kits.
  Close the panel to resume movement and combat. Opening it cancels aim and
    movement orders; it does not pause the enemy or interrupt a committed cast.
  Switching requires a living hero at the checkpoint and ten quiet seconds
    after dealing or receiving external damage (including shield absorption).
  Active aim, cast, dash, returning sword, R recast, trail, shockwave, crowd
    control, guard, shield and warcry also block swapping. The panel explains
    the current lock. A cooldown alone does not block a legal switch.
  Swapping never changes a slot's cooldown deadline, including W during a
    mixed-kit empowered R dash. This test unlock/loadout lasts for the current
    Play session and survives normal death and Y reset in the combat sandbox.
    Milestone 3 also saves this test unlock and loadout in Lvl_CryptSlice.
    A dungeon-earned ascension unlock remains later scope.

Controls retained from Milestone 1
  Click/hold click: constant-speed navigation. Hold Q/W/E/R to preview;
    release to cast. Aiming permits movement; committed windups stop movement.
  Right click: cancel aim/attack/movement. Space: repeat nearest/hover-selected
    basic attack. F: up-to-6 m Flash, 60 s cooldown. L: checkpoint menu.
  T: place an Elite dummy at the cursor. J: 120 raw physical self-test damage.
  Y: development reset of health, cooldowns, encounter and owned effects.
    It deliberately bypasses combat locks, while retaining equipped abilities.
  Normal defeat: checkpoint respawn after 4 s with 3 s spawn protection;
    cooldown deadlines continue through death. Hostile commitments end spawn
    protection. See MILESTONE-1-TEST.txt for the original kit and enemy rules.

Abyss abilities (raw values, before resistance and damage reduction)
  Q / Greatsword Quake
    2.5 m, 90-degree cone; 150 physical damage; 0.35 s cast; 6 s cooldown.
    Releases a piercing, terrain-blocked 8 m shockwave at 14 m/s, width 1.5 m,
    dealing 80 magic damage. Direct cone targets cannot also take wave damage.
    A direct Elite hit refunds 5 s of current R cooldown once per cast.
    Shockwave-only hits do not refund R. Cone and wave have separate previews.
  W / Abyss Dash
    Up to 6 m along legal terrain at 15 m/s; 0.65 m swept radius; 80 physical
    damage once per target; 0.10 s windup; 12 s cooldown. The speed preserves
    the slower W requested during Milestone 1.
    Leaves a 1.5 m wide trail. Each section lasts 4 s from placement, dealing
    20 magic damage each second, first tick after one second. Overlapping
    sections from one dash do not multiply ticks; no DOT remains after exit.
    Ordinary W provides no ultimate healing.
  E / Abyss Guard
    33% damage reduction for 6 s; instant self-buff; 30 s cooldown.
    Every full 100 actual HP lost while active refunds 1 s of current R
    cooldown. Fractional HP loss accumulates during that guard and expires
    with it. Shields, immunity and overkill do not increase the refund.
  R / Abyss Warcry
    0.40 s cast; 4 m self-centered circle; 260 magic damage; 120 s cooldown.
    Capsule edges count, and walls block enemy hits. Grants +33% walking and
    attack speed for 10 s: 665 cm/s walking, 0.752 s basic-attack cycle, 0.135 s
    basic windup. Casts, dashes and Flash retain their fixed speeds/timings.
    Warcry grants no returning sword, empowered W recast or ultimate healing.

Mixed-kit original R
  Original R still throws the returning sword. Its direction is fixed when
    the aim key is released, as in the accepted Milestone 1 baseline.
  Catching it opens a 2 s recast; the recast empowers the CURRENTLY EQUIPPED W.
  Original W empowered: 8 m at 22 m/s; radius 1 m; 180 physical damage.
  Abyss W empowered: 8 m at 22 m/s; radius 0.8 m; 140 physical damage;
    2 m wide trail, each section 5 s, 30 magic damage per second.
  Both have a 0.10 s windup and leave ordinary W cooldown untouched.
  Original R's sword, empowered direct W and empowered Abyss trail heal
    20% of actual HP damage dealt, capped by missing health and using the
    shared healing modifiers. Trail healing continues after the recast window.
  Released sword/shockwave/trail damage can finish after caster death. Death
    permanently disables healing/recast benefits for that released effect,
    even if the same pawn revives before its remaining hits/ticks finish.

Shared values
  Physical/magic damage uses armor/resistance, then multiplicative damage
    reduction (75% cap), then shields, then remaining HP. Refunds/healing use
    HP actually removed. Healing cannot revive a defeated actor.
  Outgoing and incoming healing bonuses add within each category (50% cap),
    then apply the strongest anti-heal (75% cap).
  Ability haste uses base cooldown * 100 / (100 + haste), capped at 100 haste.
    It is recorded when the cooldown starts; changing haste or a loadout does
    not recalculate existing cooldowns. Durations, casts, trail ticks, recast
    windows, auto attacks and Flash are not shortened by ability haste.
  Sword color follows equipped Q. The purple aura follows equipped Abyss R.
  Ability constants are in Source/Mistworld/MistAlphonseAbilityDefinitions.cpp;
    the controller selects a definition for each equipped slot.

Useful play checks
  - At spawn, press L, unlock Abyss, select all four alternate abilities.
    Close the menu and hold Q: both the short cone and long wave are shown.
    Walk while aiming; release to commit. Put one dummy close and one beyond
    the cone: they receive direct and wave damage respectively.
  - Dash through a dummy with Abyss W. It takes one direct hit; leaving it
    standing on the trail adds delayed ticks. Try returning to the checkpoint
    before the trail expires: the menu should explain why switching is locked.
  - Use Abyss E, then J. With 20 armor, each J removes 67 HP under guard; two
    such hits cross the 100 HP threshold and refund 1 s of current R cooldown.
    An existing R cooldown is needed to see the refund in the top HUD.
  - Warcry near several dummies, then move and use Space. Walking and attacks
    speed up for 10 s. Normal constant-speed starts/stops are retained.
  - Select original R and Abyss W. Damage yourself, throw/catch the sword,
    and recast R through a dummy. The direct hit and subsequent trail ticks
    heal you; ordinary W does not. R's preview includes the empowered trail.
  - Use an ability without hitting an enemy, wait for its active effects to
    finish, then switch its slot while its cooldown still runs. The displayed
    remaining time must remain unchanged.
  - Deal damage, return to the gold circle, and open L. Wait for ten seconds
    since the last actual damage event; the menu then permits swaps.
