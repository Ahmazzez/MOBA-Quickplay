Movement
  Left click: navigate around obstacles at 500 cm/s.
  Starts, turns, and arrival stops are immediate, with no acceleration or
    braking. Fixed destinations have at most one frame of position tolerance.
  Holding a movement click updates the navigation destination continuously.
  Q's preview clips its legal outline at terrain and marks blocked range red.
    Dash/sword previews include both rounded ends of the swept hitbox.
  Movement continues while holding an ability preview. Committed Q, W, R,
    empowered R, and basic attack wind-ups stop movement.
  E is an instant self-buff and does not stop movement.

Controls
  Hold Q / W / E / R: preview; move the cursor to aim; release to cast.
    Only one preview is active. Selecting another ready slot replaces it.
    Right click cancels without spending cooldown.
  Q: 3 m / 110-degree cleave, 140 physical damage, 0.25 s wind-up, 6 s CD.
    Hitting at least one Elite dummy refunds 5 s of R cooldown once per cast.
  W: dash up to 6 m along its legal path at 15 m/s; 0.75 m swept radius;
    100 physical damage once per target; 0.10 s wind-up; 12 s CD; no heal.
  E: 50% damage reduction after armor for 5 s; 30 s CD; self indicator.
  R: 10 m returning sword, 0.60 m swept radius, 18 m/s; 160 physical damage
    once per target on each leg; 0.35 s wind-up; 120 s CD. The direction is
    fixed when the aim key is released. Return follows your current position.
    Hitting a wall starts the return immediately; catching the sword still
    opens a 2 s R recast window.
  Hold/release R during RECAST: empowered dash up to 8 m at 22 m/s,
    radius 1 m, 180 physical damage once per target, 0.10 s wind-up.
    It consumes the recast without spending or resetting ordinary W's CD.
    The sword and empowered dash heal 20% of actual HP damage dealt.
  F: instant cursor-directed Flash, up to 6 m, 60 s CD.
    Root/stun prevent Flash; silence does not. Terrain/navigation can shorten
    the endpoint. A completely invalid endpoint does not consume cooldown.
  Space: select the closest living enemy in basic-attack range and repeat
    attacks (90 raw damage, 1 s cycle, 0.18 s wind-up). An enemy under the
    cursor overrides nearest targeting; an explicit out-of-range target is
    approached first. Automatic targeting excludes targets behind walls.
    Click elsewhere or right-click to stop attacking.
  T over clear navigable floor: place an Elite training dummy.
  Y: development reset for player/dummy health, cooldowns, buffs and recasts;
    the Sentinel also returns to its original home and clears its AI state.
  J: apply 120 raw physical test damage to yourself (100 normally, 50 under E).
  Escape: ends the editor's Play session under its default shortcut.

First enemy encounter
  The separate 40 m flat arena has a red Sentinel and a passive practice dummy.
  Enter the Sentinel's 8 m ring, or damage it from within its 14 m home leash.
  It approaches at 2.5 m/s. In melee range it shows an orange 2.5 m / 100-degree
    attack sector for 0.8 s. Its facing locks when the warning begins.
  Move sideways out of the sector before impact, then use its 1.2 s recovery
    window to attack. The Sentinel does not move or attack during recovery.
  Its swing deals 100 raw physical damage (83.33 after Alphonse's 20 armor;
    41.67 during E). Terrain blocks hits. Stun or defeat cancels its swing.
  The Sentinel has 900 HP and 20 armor/resistance. All current abilities and
    Space attacks can damage it. Killing it leaves it down until Y reset.
  Leave the home leash to disengage. It returns home immune to damage/debuffs
    and restores health only after reaching home. Y resets immediately.
  At zero HP, Alphonse stops, cancels pending casts/recasts and revives at his
    starting checkpoint after 4 s. The top HUD shows the respawn countdown.
    Normal death/revive keeps all cooldown deadlines; they continue ticking.

Death and spawn protection
  Initial spawn and every normal revive grant 3 s damage/debuff immunity,
    shown as SPAWN GUARD in the HUD and a cyan ring around the hero.
  Walking, holding/cancelling an aim preview, self-healing, E and F preserve
    the protection. A valid committed Q/W/R/recast/basic attack ends only the
    spawn protection; unrelated immunity sources remain intact.
  Invalid casts and empty attack orders never spend protection or cooldown.
  Death clears temporary combat effects and pending input/casts/recasts,
    disables movement, and restores any temporary dash collision change.
    The released sword keeps its damage and attribution, but loses healing
    and recast benefits for this and later lives of the same pawn.
  The checkpoint must safely fit the hero's capsule; a blocked checkpoint
    retries rather than reviving inside an obstacle.
  Y is a development reset: resets cooldowns and the encounter, removes
    released swords, and immediately revives a dead hero with spawn guard.
    Using Y while alive clears temporary effects without moving the hero.

  - While holding Q/W/R, click new points. Preview follows the moving hero.

Damage/health rules
  Physical damage uses armor; magic uses magic resistance. Both start at 20.
  Distinct damage reductions multiply, capped at 75%; same named effects
    retain the strongest value and refresh duration.
  Healing bonuses add per outgoing/incoming category (each capped at +50%);
    strongest anti-heal applies afterward (capped at 75%). Healing clamps to
    missing HP, uses actual damage removed, and cannot revive a dead caster.
  Root, stun, silence, damage immunity and debuff immunity are separate timed
    states. They are available for upcoming enemies/encounters and tests.
