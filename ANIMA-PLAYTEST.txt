MISTWORLD - ANIMA & CHARACTER SWITCHING

Start at a safe crypt checkpoint. Press L, then choose Anima.
Use Unlock Flamestealer / test access to enable her alternate skills.
Choose each original or ascension Q/W/E/R independently, then close L.
I opens the selected hero's equipment and model preview.

Q/W/E/R: hold to preview, release to commit. Aiming allows movement.
F: Flash (60 seconds). Space: auto-attack closest enemy in reach;
hovering a different enemy overrides that target selection.
B: replay the boss at either safe checkpoint. Y: development reset.
T: create a training target at the cursor. J: development self-damage.

Anima original kit:
Q Mark: rapier thrust, 4-second sole mark, heal from damage to marked target.
W Duelist Steps: three separately aimed dashes; one 5%-max-HP heal per dash
that contacts an eligible enemy. Finish the three casts or let recasts expire.
E Riposte: 1-second stance, 70% damage reduction, counter first direct hit.
Counter healing is 15%, or 30% against her mark (not 45%).
R Anima Stealer: long dash stopping at first enemy, mark, silence and heal theft.
Boss silence/theft immunity is respected.
Passive: time a rapier auto swing to parry a frontal enemy basic attack;
blocks one attack and heals its would-be damage, at most once per 3 seconds.

Flamestealer alternatives:
Q Brand: applies a 4-second brand; autos apply independently timed burns.
W Ember Steps: three dashes apply two burns each, healing from burn stacks
already on the first non-minion enemy hit (up to 5% max HP per dash).
E Flame Stance: parry one damage event, then 5 seconds of +20% auto damage
and burn application. An auto on a branded target adds two burns total.
R Inflamed Soul: 8-second nearby burn aura; heal from own burn damage
to non-minions while active. Mark can also heal in a mixed loadout.

Each hero keeps their own loadout, cooldowns, and health within the session.
Hero/loadout changes are blocked during combat, active encounters, crowd
control, recasts and ongoing combat effects. Inactive cooldowns keep ticking.
Saved v1/v2 Alphonse progress is migrated without erasing earned gear.
Alphonse's greatsword reward remains his; Anima uses her own rapier, dagger,
and light-armor slots. Those equipment slots are empty in this prototype.
Weapon models remain fixed placeholders regardless of equipment.
