The creator identifies the model and included texture assets as CC0. Commercial
use and modification are permitted. Attribution is optional and retained here
as a courtesy. Seamless rock textures are by Joao Paulo via 3dtextures.me, CC0.
Carved stone normal and occlusion maps are included with the creator's model.

Mistworld preparation uses the rigged Pok4.fbx source with 9,680 triangles,
27 bones, and 3 material slots. Original UVs and vertex skin weights are retained.
The model is normalized to two metres, with its lowest reference point at Z=0.

Four simple prototype animation clips were authored for this integration: idle,
walk, a two-arm slam, and collapse. These are not downloaded motion-capture clips.
Attack damage, hitboxes, and crowd control remain controlled by the game code.

Native Blender facing: -Y. Unreal FBX import: convert scene and scene units,
force front X axis, scale1.0, preserve_local_transform=False for animation.
Normal maps use the OpenGL convention; flip their green channel for Unreal.
