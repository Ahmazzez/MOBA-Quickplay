Mistworld - Alphonse UI polish

Visual direction
  Bright fantasy: ivory panels, blue accents, gold borders and ornaments.
  The combat dock sits at the bottom of the viewport. It shows health, status,
  Q/W/E/R, F for Flash, and Space for basic attacks. Each skill has a distinct
  vector icon, key label and numerical cooldown over a filling overlay.
  The objective and checkpoint interaction hint remain at the upper left.

Inventory (I)
  Select one of Alphonse's five equipment slots:
    Greatsword Blade
    Greatsword Guard
    Heavy Helmet
    Heavy Chestplate
    Heavy Boots
  The center shows the actual current character mesh and attached sword.
  Use < and > to rotate the preview, or Reset view to restore its initial view.
  The preview is cosmetic: rotating it does not turn or move the player.
  It stops updating when the inventory closes and is cleaned up with the HUD.
  When Alphonse's mannequin is replaced, the preview copies the replacement
  mesh/materials and any attached static or skeletal equipment automatically.

  Select Greatsword Blade to equip/unequip the earned Necrotic Greatsword.
  This existing reward adds 5% ability damage, without changing basic attacks.
  The other four slots are empty until their equipment is implemented.
  They do not duplicate the blade's stats or invent additional loot.
  Equipment changes still require a safe checkpoint, ten quiet seconds and
  no unfinished encounter or active effects. The details panel explains locks.
  Inventory and loadout are mutually exclusive. They do not pause enemies.

Loadout (L)
  The ascension/loadout screen uses matching ivory, blue and gold styling.
  Existing independent Original/Abyss selections and switching rules remain.

Saved progression
  Legacy version-1 saves migrate to version 2 with the Blade slot name.
  Completion, ownership, equipment, checkpoint, mixed loadout, cooldowns and
  the stale-write revision are preserved. The filename intentionally stays
  Saved/SaveGames/Mistworld.Crypt.v1.sav so existing progress is found.

Play checks
  1. Open Mistworld.uproject normally, then press Play in Lvl_CryptSlice.
  2. Click the viewport for focus. Confirm health and skills are at the bottom.
     Hold Q while moving; release and observe its numerical cooldown.
  3. At the entrance, press I. Check all five slot names and the real model.
     Rotate/reset it, select each empty slot, and close with I or Close I.
  4. After earning the chest reward, select Blade and equip it at a safe
     checkpoint. Stop/restart Play and confirm the saved equipment persists.
  5. Press L and confirm the alternate-kit controls still work.
  6. Resize the viewport; panels and their click regions should scale together.

Art workflow
  Read Mistworld-Art-Guide.txt in this project. Start with a 2 m floor tile or
  a separate greatsword blade and guard before building the whole crypt or
  a rigged hero. Export FBX, import into a separate Art folder, and compare
  dimensions against the mannequin. The existing gameplay capsule, movement,
  navigation and ability timings remain the reference during art integration.
