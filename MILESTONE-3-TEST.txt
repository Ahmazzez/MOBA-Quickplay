Mistworld - Milestone 3: one playable crypt dungeon slice

Start
  Open E:\Unreal Projects\Mistworld\Mistworld.uproject normally in Unreal.
  Open /Game/Mistworld/Maps/Lvl_CryptSlice and press Play.
  Click the viewport for keyboard focus. This is a single-player dungeon with
    placeholder mannequin, sword, guardian shapes and dungeon art.
  The existing /Game/Mistworld/Maps/Lvl_CombatTest remains the combat sandbox.

Controls
  Click/hold click: constant-speed navigation. Right click: cancel orders/aim.
  Hold Q/W/E/R: aim; release: cast. Moving while aiming remains supported.
  Space: repeat basic attack, nearest enemy unless the cursor selects another.
  F: Flash, up to 6 m, 60 s cooldown. L: checkpoint loadout. I: inventory.
  C: claim the chest reward while inside the reward checkpoint after victory.
  L and I open mutually exclusive menus and stop new movement/attack orders.
    They do not pause enemies. Close the menu before continuing combat.
  Prototype Unlock Abyss remains available in L at a safe checkpoint. The
    unlock and four individual ability selections now save in the crypt.
  T: training dummy; J: self-test damage; Y: development reset. Y deliberately
    resets cooldowns and health, returns you to the active checkpoint and
    clears an unfinished encounter. It retains earned loot and completion.

Encounter
  You start at the gold entrance checkpoint in the west foyer. Walk east
    through the opening to start the fight. Its gate closes during the attempt.
  Ember (orange cube), Tide (blue sphere) and Stone (green cone) guard the
    golem. Their matching seals disappear as each guardian is defeated.
  Guardians inside the blue 7 m protective circle have 75% damage reduction.
    Bait a charge outside it and attack during the six-second recovery.
  Each charge shows its locked direction and lane for 1.2 s before moving.
    It travels up to 8.5 m at 9 m/s and deals 120 raw physical damage once.
    Dodge sideways after the tell. Walls stop a charge; units do not block it.
  Kill all three guardians to expose the golem for 15 s. If it survives, it
    summons a fresh wave while retaining the health damage already dealt.
  The exposed golem uses a locked 110-degree, 2.5 m cone with a 1.1 s tell,
    180 raw physical damage and 1.8 s recovery. Step out of the shown cone.
  Guardian base health is 300; golem health is 3000. Both have 20 armor and
    magic resistance. The golem is immune while protected. Guardian charges
    provide the intended escape from protection; displacement cannot do so.
  Loadout, equipment and checkpoint changes are blocked during an active
    attempt, even if you have avoided damage for ten seconds.
  Defeat returns you to the active checkpoint after four seconds with three
    seconds of spawn protection. An unfinished fight resets. Released sword,
    wave and trail effects from a wiped attempt are removed. Normal death
    preserves cooldown deadlines; respawning does not refresh abilities.

Reward and saved progression
  Victory opens the gate and activates the northeast reward checkpoint.
  Enter its gold circle and wait for ten quiet seconds plus the end of active
    effects. Press C near the chest to claim the Necrotic Greatsword once.
  Press I, select Greatsword Blade and click Equip blade. It adds 5% to
    Alphonse's ability damage, including
    released sword hits, shockwaves and trail ticks. Basic attacks retain
    their original damage. Equip/unequip requires a safe checkpoint.
  The crypt saves completion, reward ownership, equipment, checkpoint,
    Abyss unlock, mixed loadout and the remaining six combat cooldowns.
  Autosaves occur every two seconds and on changes/normal Play shutdown.
    Cooldowns continue to elapse during time spent outside the game.
  Resume after stopping Play or restarting Unreal: a cleared crypt remains
    cleared, you return to the saved checkpoint, and equipment remains equipped.
    The chest cannot award a duplicate. This slice represents one saved clear;
    repeatable dungeon runs are later scope.
  Save location: Saved/SaveGames/Mistworld.Crypt.v1.sav in this project.
    The filename is retained for compatibility. The current data version is 2;
    version-1 saves migrate automatically to the Greatsword Blade slot while
    preserving earned progress, loadout, cooldowns and the save revision.
    It is a versioned local prototype save, separate from online progression.
    Missing or malformed saves start safely at the entrance. Failed writes
    do not grant/equip loot or activate a new checkpoint. A stale save cannot
    replace newer earned progress. The HUD displays save status.

Useful play checks
  - Cross the foyer opening. Confirm the gate closes and all three guardians
    appear. Walk while holding Q, dodge their shown lanes, then attack outside
    the protective circle. Observe each matching seal going dark on death.
  - Defeat all three. Attack the golem during its 15 s opening while dodging
    the cone. Let a window expire: a new wave appears without healing the boss.
  - Lose one attempt. Confirm checkpoint respawn, immunity, reset guardians,
    no surviving trail damage, and cooldowns still counting down.
  - Win, claim and equip the reward at the northeast checkpoint. Stop Play,
    start again, then restart Unreal normally and start again. Confirm your
    checkpoint/loadout/loot and cleared state persist without a second reward.

Automated acceptance
  Mistworld.Crypt.PlayableSlice uses the real generated map and two separate
    Play sessions with an isolated temporary save. It checks navigation,
    encounter entry/wipe, checkpoint revival, reward/equipment, exact 5%
    ability scaling, unchanged basic damage and restored progress/cooldowns.
  Mistworld.Dungeon tests guardian protection/telegraphs/collision and golem
    phases. Mistworld.Progression tests persistence, corruption, write failure
    and stale-save protection. Tests never use the player's crypt save.
  Earlier combat, movement, targeting, loadout and mixed-kit tests remain part
    of the full Mistworld suite. See Saved/Automation/Milestone3Final/index.json
    and Saved/Logs/Milestone3Tests.log for the verified build's results.
